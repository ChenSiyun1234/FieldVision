Corn silage vs grain, field by field

FieldVision reads each corn field's harvest from Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery, calls it silage or grain (or leaves it unjudged), adds the calls up by county and checks them against USDA NASS. Figures are model estimates, not NASS statistics.

How far the estimates can be trusted

Labelled US field-years (Beltsville, MD)42 of 44 right; always-grain gets 32
Training data8,000 labelled European fields; no US labels used
Clean tests (run once, rules written first)4 of 5 within 5 points of USDA: Michigan, New York and Wisconsin 2025, Lancaster 2023; Pennsylvania 2025 missed
Known weak spotNorthern silage cut after the crop yellows reads as grain (Marathon, WI)
USDA referenceCensus 2012, 2017, 2022 by county; NASS 2025 survey by state (6.19 M silage acres)
